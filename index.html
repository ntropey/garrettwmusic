About

More Than Background Music

Garrett W brings decades of performance experience to every event — from intimate solo piano to a full acoustic trio with vocals. Rooted in The Great American Songbook with a broad reach into contemporary favorites, the sound is warm, versatile, and unmistakably live.


Based on Maryland's Eastern Shore, Garrett W Music has performed at weddings, engagement celebrations, private gatherings, and corporate events throughout the Chesapeake Bay region.
